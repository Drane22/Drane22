### `> whoami`

I like building at the intersection of **design, technology, and everyday problems**. Sometimes that means exploring music through album-cover aesthetics; other times it's making QR codes into miniature 3D worlds, improving patient workflows, or giving an e-paper display a new purpose.

My favorite projects make complicated ideas feel **simple, visual, and fun to use**.

```text
CURRENT SIGNAL
├─ Creative interfaces  → music, generative art, interactive worlds
├─ Useful systems      → patient workflows, data, accessibility
└─ Hardware + tooling  → e-paper, Bluetooth, small experiments
```

### `> selected_projects`

| **[Articol](https://github.com/Drane22/Articol)** · [Live demo ↗](https://articol-lime.vercel.app) | **Album discovery through visual similarity.** Uses CLIP embeddings, color science, composition, and typography to find records that *look* related, plus algorithmic palette art. |
